**Status: defunct (2016) — no API surface.**

Brewster was a New York mobile contacts startup founded by Steve Greenwood. Its iOS app, launched in July 2012, merged phone contacts with Google Apps, Facebook, Twitter, Foursquare and LinkedIn into a single self-updating, deduplicated address book, surfacing relationship signals about who a user actually engaged with.

Backed by Union Square Ventures and Slow Ventures.

- **October 2015** — the founding team was acqui-hired by Restaurant Brands International (Burger King / Tim Hortons). The app and its data did not transfer.
- **March 2016** — FullContact acquired Brewster's self-updating address book technology and shut the consumer product down within 30 days.

Brewster never published a public developer API, SDK, OpenAPI definition, or developer portal. No API artifacts exist for this repo; it is retained as a historical network record.

Note: `brewster.com` no longer belongs to this company — it redirects to `brewsterwallcovering.com`, an unrelated wallcovering manufacturer.
